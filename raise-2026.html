Methodology:
• Company Already Engaged = Salesforce shows an existing Opportunity (any owner/team), an existing Contact, or another open Lead tied to that company's domain, from OUTSIDE this RAISE booth-scan interaction — i.e., someone at that company was already talking to us before/regardless of who we met at the booth.
• Qualified = real Salesforce Lead Status of "In Progress (MQL)" or "In Progress (SAL)" — not an estimate.
• Potential Pipeline = $200,000 × the number of Qualified leads (6 MQL + 7 SAL = 13), hardcoded flat per lead regardless of any real Salesforce Opportunity. Neysa and Neysa.ai (same domain, two Salesforce Lead records) are counted once, so SAL is 7, not 8.
• Mobile / LinkedIn / Notes are pulled live from Salesforce (Lead.MobilePhone, Lead.LinkedIn__c, Lead.Lead_Notes__c).
• Data refreshes automatically every 6 hours via the clockwork-sfdc-sync GitHub Action; re-open this page to see the latest snapshot as of generation time.
